# ResearchGate Publication Blurbs

## Opus I

**Polarized Membrane Cosmology, Opus I: Branch-Relative Matter Asymmetry and a Topological Relic Channel**  
DOI: `10.5281/zenodo.20991445`

Introduces a three-sector membrane-yield framework in which a higher-dimensional balanced state produces a visible matter-oriented branch, a conjugate branch, and a neutral residual relic sector. The note preserves local QED and gauge neutrality and frames dark matter as a possible relic output of the same transition.

## Opus II

**Polarized Sl-Membrane Cosmology, Opus II: Universal Orientation Sorting and a Geometric Yield Ansatz**  
DOI: `10.5281/zenodo.20991835`

Names the branch-separating membrane `Sl` and sharpens the orientation-sorting picture. The note identifies the dark-to-baryonic matter ratio as the first quantitative target and outlines the need for a geometric or index-theoretic relic-yield calculation.

## Opus III

**Polarized Sl-Membrane Cosmology, Opus III: Universal Matter Orientation and Relic Statistics**  
DOI: `10.5281/zenodo.21312037`

Reframes matter/antimatter orientation as a universal pre-particle label, with observed particles treated as projection modes. Clarifies that the `~40 MeV` scale is the estimated branch-separation epoch inferred from the observed matter/photon ratio, not a present-day barrier. Establishes the relic-statistics constraint for the dark sector.

## Conjecture I

**Conjecture I: Xenon Recoil as a Constraint on Cold Sl-Membrane Relics**

A short technical note connecting Opus III to possible detector constraints. It does not claim that current xenon data prove dark matter. It defines the cold-relic requirement, the relic-statistics equation, and the first sanity check: a naive one-scale Sl scalar condensate overproduces dark matter by roughly `10^6--10^7`.
