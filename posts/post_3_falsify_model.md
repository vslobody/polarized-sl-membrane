# How to falsify the membrane model

The point of a speculative model is not to sound grand. The point is to become easy to kill.

The polarized Sl-membrane model fails if any of the following are true:

1. The `T_c ~ 38--40 MeV` transition scale cannot be obtained from annihilation timing and the observed matter/photon ratio.
2. Branch separation cannot preserve Standard Model gauge neutrality.
3. The model collapses into ordinary matter/antimatter domains in our universe, which are observationally excluded.
4. The neutral relic sector behaves like radiation and violates `N_eff`, BBN, or CMB constraints.
5. The relic sector cannot satisfy:

   ```text
   m_R n_R ~= 5.3 m_p n_b
   ```

6. The relic-to-baryon statistic `kappa_R = n_R/n_b` cannot be derived.
7. Any required nuclear-recoil portal is already excluded by direct detection, gas-cloud heating, accretion disks, neutron stars, or structure formation.
8. The model requires local positrons to be absent from our branch, contradicting QED.

Current first negative result: a naive one-scale Sl scalar condensate with `m_phi ~ phi_0 ~ T_c ~ 40 MeV` overproduces dark matter by roughly `10^6--10^7`.

That does not kill the framework, but it wounds the simplest dark-relic version.
