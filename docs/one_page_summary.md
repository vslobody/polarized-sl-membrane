# Membrane Model — One-Page Entry Note

## Status

Conjectural framework. Not established physics. The purpose is to define a testable idea clearly enough that it can be attacked or excluded.

## Core claim

The observed matter/antimatter imbalance may be branch-relative rather than global. A deeper balanced state separates into two conjugate branches during a Sl-membrane phase transition.

```text
H_deep -> H_A + H_B + R_Sl
```

- `H_A`: our visible matter-oriented branch.
- `H_B`: conjugate branch carrying the opposite orientation.
- `R_Sl`: neutral residual Sl relic sector produced by the separation event.

## What problem it addresses

Standard cosmology observes:

```text
eta_B ~ 6e-10
Omega_DM / Omega_b ~ 5.3
```

The model asks whether these two numbers could be related to one early branch-separation event.

## What it preserves

- Local QED: positrons and pair creation remain allowed.
- Standard Model gauge neutrality in the visible branch.
- No ordinary matter/antimatter domains inside our observable universe.
- Global baryon number can remain zero across conjugate branches.

## What it changes

- Matter/antimatter orientation is treated as a deeper pre-particle label.
- Particles are projection modes of a deeper state.
- The visible matter excess is the residue remaining when Sl separation interrupts cross-orientation annihilation.

## Why 40 MeV matters

The universal orientation label answers: **what is separated?**

The Sl transition temperature answers: **when does separation become irreversible?**

Before Sl condensation, annihilation continues. When Sl condenses, the remaining matter-oriented and conjugate-oriented populations are branch-separated. The observed matter/photon ratio acts as the clock:

```text
eta_B ~ 6e-10 -> T_c ~ 38--40 MeV
```

## Dark sector

If the neutral Sl relic sector is dark matter, it must satisfy:

```text
m_R n_R ~= 5.3 m_p n_b
```

or:

```text
m_R ~= (5.3 / kappa_R) m_p,   kappa_R = n_R/n_b
```

The key open number is `kappa_R`.

## First constraint

A naive scalar condensate with all scales set by the transition,

```text
m_phi ~ phi_0 ~ T_c ~ 40 MeV
```

overproduces dark matter by roughly `10^6--10^7`. Therefore the relic sector needs a suppressed amplitude, lighter mode, compact relic statistics, or another derived scale.

## Observable handles

- Rare nuclear recoils in xenon-type detectors.
- Neutron-star heating/capture constraints.
- Black-hole-adjacent events or relic instability.
- Ultra-high-energy particles from early violent relaxation.
- BBN/CMB/`N_eff` limits on radiation-like relics.

## Failure conditions

The model fails if it cannot preserve gauge neutrality, cannot derive the 40 MeV timing, cannot produce a cold relic sector with the correct density, or requires a portal interaction already excluded by direct detection or astrophysics.
